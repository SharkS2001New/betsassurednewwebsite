## One Million HT/FT Predictions

HT/FT One Million prediction are the half-time/full-time market. This market predicts the result at half-time and the final result at full-time.

The combinations include Home/Home, Draw/Home, Draw/Draw, Away/Away and Away/Draw. For example, Draw/Home predicts that the match will be level at half-time before the home team wins at full-time.
