## 1960 Tips Double Chance Predictions

1960 tips does not always predict one team to win outright. Double Chance covers two possible results within the same pick.

The three options are:

- **1X:** Home win or draw.
- **X2:** Away win or draw.
- **12:** Home win or away win, excluding a draw.

For example, a 1X prediction covers a home victory and a drawn match. It loses if the away team wins. The X2 option works in the opposite direction, covering an away win or draw.

1960 tips Double Chance is useful when analysing matches where the outright winner is less clear. The choice still depends on team strength, recent performances and the likelihood of a draw.
