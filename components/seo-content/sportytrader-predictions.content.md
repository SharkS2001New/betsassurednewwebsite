## SportyTrader Predictions

SportyTrader predictions provide football tips for matches across different leagues and competitions. The predictions cover possible match winners, goal totals, exact scores and other betting markets.

The main **types of bets covered** include:

- **1X2:** Home win, draw or away win.
- **Double chance:** 1X, X2 or 12, covering two possible match outcomes.
- **Over/under goals:** Predictions based on the total number of goals scored.
- **Exact scores:** Predictions for specific final scores, such as 1–0 or 2–1.
- **Half-time/full-time:** Predictions combining the result at half-time with the final result.
- **Both Teams to Score (BTTS):** Whether both teams will score at least one goal.
- **Draw predictions:** Matches where the teams could finish level.

Each betting market has different conditions. A correct score prediction, for example, requires a more specific outcome than a standard 1X2 prediction.

## SportyTrader 1X2 Predictions

SportyTrader 1X2 predictions cover three possible results: a home win (1), a draw (X) or an away win (2).

These predictions focus on the final match result rather than the exact score. For example, a 1–0 and a 3–1 result both count as home wins.

## SportyTrader Double Chance Predictions

SportyTrader double chance predictions cover two possible 1X2 outcomes. The available options are 1X for a home win or draw, X2 for an away win or draw, and 12 for either team to win.

Double chance differs from a standard match-winner prediction because it includes two possible results. The odds and conditions depend on the selected market.

## SportyTrader Over/Under Goals Predictions

SportyTrader over/under goals predictions focus on the combined number of goals scored by both teams.

An over 2.5 goals prediction requires at least three goals in the match. Under 2.5 goals requires no more than two goals. This market differs from BTTS because it measures total goals rather than whether each team scores.

## SportyTrader Exact Score Predictions

SportyTrader exact score predictions identify a possible final score, such as 1–0, 2–1 or 2–2. These predictions require the final score to match the predicted scoreline.

Exact score predictions are more specific than match-result tips. Several scorelines can produce the same winner, which makes the exact score a separate betting market.

## SportyTrader Half-Time/Full-Time Predictions

SportyTrader half-time/full-time predictions combine two results in one market: the outcome at half-time and the outcome at full-time.

For example, 1/1 means the home team leads at half-time and wins at full-time. X/2 means the match is level at half-time and the away team wins by full-time.

This market considers how a match could develop across both halves, rather than only the final result.

## SportyTrader BTTS Predictions

SportyTrader BTTS predictions focus on whether both teams will score during a match. BTTS Yes requires each team to score at least one goal, while BTTS No means at least one team finishes without scoring.

This market differs from over/under goals. A match can have three goals without both teams scoring, such as a 3–0 result.
