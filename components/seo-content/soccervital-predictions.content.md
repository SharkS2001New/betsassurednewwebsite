## SoccerVital Over/Under Goals Predictions

SoccerVital also provides goals tips for football fixtures. Over/under predictions focus on the total number of goals scored by both teams during a match.

For example, an over 2.5 goals prediction requires at least three total goals, while under 2.5 goals requires two or fewer. This market differs from BTTS because a match can produce three goals without both teams scoring.
