Forebet over/under goals predictions help analyze whether a match will finish with more or fewer goals than a set total. The common goal lines include 1.5, 2.5 and 3.5.

The main options work as follows:

- **Over 2.5 goals:** At least three goals are scored.
- **Under 2.5 goals:** No more than two goals are scored.
- **Over 3.5 goals:** At least four goals are scored.
