## Types of Football Predictions You May See

On a complete football predictions page, you will often find more than one market. The most common are:

### 1X2 Predictions
This market covers the three main match outcomes:
- **1** = Home win
- **X** = Draw
- **2** = Away win

1X2 predictions are popular because they are simple to understand and widely available across bookmakers.

### Double Chance Predictions
Double chance combines two outcomes into one option:
- **1X** = Home win or draw
- **X2** = Draw or away win
- **12** = Home win or away win

This market can be helpful for users who prefer lower-risk options compared to straight 1X2 picks.

### Over/Under Predictions
These predictions focus on the number of goals expected in the match. Common markets include:
- Over 1.5 goals
- Over 2.5 goals
- Under 2.5 goals

Goal-based tips are especially useful when it is difficult to trust one team to win, but match trends suggest a strong scoring pattern.
