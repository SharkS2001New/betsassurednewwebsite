## Yesterday's Football Predictions: Results & Tips Review
Reviewing yesterday's football predictions is one of the most useful habits a serious bettor can develop. Seeing which tips landed, which missed, and what the final scores were gives you a concrete feedback loop against which to calibrate your own selections. This page shows all football predictions published for yesterday alongside the final scores and won/lost outcomes, so you can review tip performance across 1X2, double chance, and over/under markets in one place.

## How to Use Yesterday's Predictions for Improvement

Every match card on this page shows the original prediction made before kick-off alongside the final result. The won/lost badge next to each prediction chip reflects whether the tip came in based on the full-time score. This is a straightforward performance record — no adjusted or retroactive picks, just the original model output measured against reality.

There are two productive ways to use this data. The first is a quick daily scan: checking the overall won/lost split gives you a rolling sense of model accuracy across different fixture types and leagues. The second is deeper analysis: identifying which types of fixtures the model calls well (and which it tends to miss) helps you apply personal judgement on top of the automated tips when making your own selections.

### What yesterday's results tell you about fixture types

Not all fixture types are equally predictable. Home wins in strong domestic leagues where the home side is a significant favourite tend to convert at higher rates than away wins in evenly matched cup ties. Double chance tips carry higher hit rates by design but at lower odds. Over/Under 2.5 tips in Bundesliga fixtures tend to land more reliably than the same market in defensive leagues. Reviewing yesterday's results over time makes these patterns visible.

When a tip misses, the score tells the story. A predicted home win that ended 0–0 is a very different miss than a 2–3 away comeback — the first suggests an overestimation of home attacking output, the second an underestimation of away quality. These distinctions matter when you're assessing whether a similar fixture type tomorrow merits the same confidence level.

### Using the won/lost record in accumulator review

If you placed an accumulator using yesterday's tips, this page lets you trace exactly where the slip stood before the last leg and identify which specific fixture caused it to fail — or which one came through to seal the win. Over multiple days, tracking which legs of your accumulators succeed and which fall apart is the most direct route to improving leg selection.

The probability rings for each fixture remain visible on this page even after the fact. Comparing the pre-match probability distribution to the actual result is a useful calibration check: a fixture where the home ring showed 70% but ended in an away win is a low-probability upset, not a model failure. A fixture where all three rings clustered near 33% and the result could have gone any way is exactly what the probability spread predicted.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How far back do yesterday's predictions go?** This page always shows the immediately preceding calendar day's fixture list. For historical results beyond yesterday, the site's results archive covers previous dates.

**Are the predictions shown exactly what was published before the matches?** Yes — the tips displayed reflect the model's pre-match output using the same data and logic that would have been visible on the day. Nothing is altered after the fact.

**Why do some matches show no won/lost badge?** A won/lost badge only appears when a final score has been recorded for the fixture. If a match was postponed, abandoned, or the result data has not yet updated, no badge appears and the score column will show a dash.

**What counts as a won tip for double chance and over/under?** For double chance (1X, X2, 12), the tip is marked won if the final result falls within the two covered outcomes. For over/under, the tip is marked won if the total goals at full time meet the threshold — three or more goals for Over 2.5, two or fewer for Under 2.5. All outcomes are measured at full time only, excluding extra time and penalties.

**How is the overall accuracy calculated?** Accuracy shown across this page is a simple win rate: tips marked Won divided by total tips with a recorded result. It reflects raw model accuracy before any personal selection or odds filtering. Your own results may differ depending on which tips you chose to back.

**Can I use this page to check if my bets settled correctly?** Yes — the final scores shown here can be used to verify settlement. If your bookmaker has settled a bet differently to what the score on this page shows, the discrepancy is worth querying with the bookmaker directly.
