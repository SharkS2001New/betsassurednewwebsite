Predictz prediction analysis often starts with the 1X2 market, which covers a home win, a draw or an away win. These three outcomes provide a simple way to assess the likely result of a football match.

The main options are:

- **1:** Home team wins.
- **X:** The match ends in a draw.
- **2:** Away team wins.
