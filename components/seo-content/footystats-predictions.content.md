## FootyStats HT/FT Predictions and Half-Time Results

FootyStats football predictions also cover half-time and full-time markets. HT/FT combines the result at half-time with the final match outcome.

Common combinations include:

- 1/1: Home team leads at half-time and wins.
- X/1: Match is level at half-time, then the home team wins.
- 2/2: Away team leads at half-time and wins.
- X/X: The match is level at half-time and full-time.

FootyStats prediction analysis for this market considers first-half goals, slow starts, late scoring and the ability to maintain a lead. These factors help compare possible outcomes, although the match can change at any point.
