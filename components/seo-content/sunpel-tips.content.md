## Types of Football Predictions on Sunpel

Sunpel covers several markets to give you a fuller picture of each day's fixtures.

### 1X2 Predictions
The most widely used football betting market covers three possible outcomes:
- **1** = Home win
- **X** = Draw
- **2** = Away win

1X2 tips are simple to understand and available across all major bookmakers, making them the most popular starting point for football bettors at every level.

### Double Chance Predictions
Double chance combines two of the three 1X2 outcomes into a single selection:
- **1X** = Home win or draw
- **X2** = Draw or away win
- **12** = Home win or away win

This market reduces risk compared to a straight win tip, making it useful when a fixture is competitive but one outcome still looks less likely than the other two.

### BTTS — Both Teams to Score
The BTTS market simply asks whether both sides will score at least once during the match. It is especially relevant in fixtures where both teams have been scoring regularly but neither is particularly dominant defensively.

### Over/Under Goals Predictions
These tips focus on the total number of goals expected across ninety minutes. The most common markets are:
- Over 1.5 goals
- Over 2.5 goals
- Under 2.5 goals

Goal-based predictions are particularly useful in matches where picking a winner is difficult but scoring trends from both sides are consistent and clear.
