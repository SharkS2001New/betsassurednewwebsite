## Types of Football Predictions on Cheerplex

Cheerplex covers several markets to give you a fuller picture of each day's fixtures.

### Home Win Predictions
A home win tip backs the home side to win the match outright. Home teams generally benefit from familiar surroundings, crowd support, and shorter travel — factors that can strengthen a direct win selection when combined with strong recent form.

### Away Win Predictions
An away win tip backs the visiting team to win outright. Away wins carry higher odds in most markets because they are less common than home wins on average, making them valuable selections when the data supports the stronger away side.

### 1X2 Predictions
The standard football betting market covers three outcomes:
- **1** = Home win
- **X** = Draw
- **2** = Away win

1X2 tips are the foundation of direct win predictions, offering a clear and straightforward market available across all major bookmakers.

### Double Chance Predictions
Double chance combines two of the three 1X2 outcomes into one selection:
- **1X** = Home win or draw
- **X2** = Draw or away win
- **12** = Home win or away win

When the data supports one team but not convincingly enough for a direct win tip, double chance offers a safer alternative that still captures the most likely outcome.

### Over/Under Goals Predictions
These tips focus on the total number of goals expected across ninety minutes:
- Over 1.5 goals
- Over 2.5 goals
- Under 2.5 goals

Goals markets are especially useful when picking a direct winner is difficult but scoring trends from both sides point clearly in one direction.
