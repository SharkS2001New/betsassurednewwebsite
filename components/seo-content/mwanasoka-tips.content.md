## Types of Football Predictions on Betsassured

Betsassured covers several markets to give you a fuller picture of each day's fixtures.

### 1X2 Predictions
The most widely used football betting market covers three possible outcomes:
- **1** = Home win
- **X** = Draw
- **2** = Away win

1X2 tips are simple to understand and available across all major bookmakers, making them the standard starting point for most football bettors.

### Double Chance Predictions
Double chance combines two of the three 1X2 outcomes into one selection:
- **1X** = Home win or draw
- **X2** = Draw or away win
- **12** = Home win or away win

This market reduces risk compared to a straight win tip and is particularly useful when a fixture is competitive but one outcome still looks significantly less likely than the other two.

### BTTS — Both Teams to Score
The BTTS market simply asks whether both sides will find the net at least once during the match. It is most relevant in fixtures where both teams have been scoring regularly but neither has been particularly solid at the back.

### Over/Under Goals Predictions
These tips focus on the total number of goals expected across the full ninety minutes. The most common markets are:
- Over 1.5 goals
- Over 2.5 goals
- Under 2.5 goals

Goal-based predictions are especially useful in matches where picking a winner is difficult but scoring trends from both sides are clear and consistent.

### Accumulator Tips
Many Betsassured users combine daily selections into accumulators. For this reason, tips are built to be usable as singles or as part of a wider accumulator strategy — giving flexibility based on your own approach to staking and risk management.
