## Types of Football Predictions on SokaFans

SokaFans covers several markets to give you a fuller picture of each day's fixtures.

### 1X2 Predictions
The most common football betting market covers three possible outcomes:
- **1** = Home win
- **X** = Draw
- **2** = Away win

1X2 tips are straightforward and widely available across all major bookmakers, making them the most popular starting point for football bettors.

### Double Chance Predictions
Double chance combines two of the three 1X2 outcomes into a single selection:
- **1X** = Home win or draw
- **X2** = Draw or away win
- **12** = Home win or away win

This market reduces risk compared to a straight win tip, making it useful when a match is competitive but one outcome still appears more probable than the third.

### BTTS — Both Teams to Score
The BTTS market asks simply whether both sides will score at least once during the match. It is especially useful in fixtures where both teams have been scoring regularly but neither is dominant defensively.

### Over/Under Goals Predictions
These tips focus on the total number of goals expected across the full ninety minutes. The most common markets are:
- Over 1.5 goals
- Over 2.5 goals
- Under 2.5 goals

Goal-based predictions are particularly useful in matches where it is difficult to predict the winner but scoring trends are consistent.
